Everything your team collects in Collect ends up in Puente. **Export** makes a copy of one form's records as a spreadsheet file — a CSV — that you can open in Excel, Google Sheets or any other spreadsheet program, and use for a report, a funder or an analyst.

The export is a copy, not a live link. Editing the spreadsheet changes nothing in Puente, and changes made in Puente afterwards do not reach a file you already downloaded. To fix a record, fix it in Manage and export again.

## Find the Export buttons

{/* claim: data-export-buttons */}
In Manage, open <UI>Manage</UI> in the left menu. The <UI>Form Manager</UI> lists your organization's forms, and each form has its own <UI>Export</UI> button: the four Puente forms at the top, then your custom forms. Every export is a CSV file.

## One file per form

{/* claim: data-one-file-per-form */}
Each button downloads one form, so you get one file per form. The Puente forms appear under their internal names:

| In Manage | In Collect | One row is |
|---|---|---|
| `SurveyData` | Resident ID | a person |
| `HistoryEnvironmentalHealth` | Environmental Health | one environmental health survey |
| `Vitals` | Vitals | one set of vitals |
| `EvaluationMedical` | Medical Evaluation | one medical evaluation |
| your custom form | your custom form | one submission of that form |

To put two files together, see [Reading export files](/docs/data/reading-export-files/).

## What the file is called

{/* claim: data-file-name */}
The file lands in your browser's downloads folder, named after the form and the moment you pressed the button, for example `SurveyData-Fri Sep 11 2026 14:40:39 GMT-0700 (Pacific Daylight Time).csv`. The name is long, but two exports of the same form never overwrite each other.

{/* claim: data-excel-bom */}
The file is marked as UTF-8, so Excel shows accented names correctly when you open it with a double-click.

## While it downloads

{/* claim: data-export-loading */}
After you press <UI>Export</UI>, the button reads <UI>Loading…</UI> until the file is ready. A large organization's export can take a while. Leave the page open and wait for the button to go back to <UI>Export</UI>.

## Empty, failed or signed out

{/* claim: data-export-messages */}
When there is no file to save, a message appears in the top corner. The three messages mean different things:

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| <UI>This form has no records to export yet.</UI> | Nothing has reached Puente on this form. No file is saved. | Nothing is wrong. Check that phones have sent their forms. |
| <UI>The export could not be completed. Please try again.</UI> | The download itself broke. Your records are fine. | Try again. If it keeps happening, [contact Puente](/docs/help/contact-support/). |
| <UI>Your sign-in has expired. Sign in again to export.</UI> | Your session ended. | Sign in again, then export. Retrying without signing in will not help. |

A file that opens with only a heading row is different again: it is a real answer meaning zero records matched, and it saves like any other file.

## What one export covers

{/* claim: data-export-aliases */}
The same organization is often written several ways in the field: its full name, an abbreviation, a spelling someone typed once on a phone. Puente keeps a list of those spellings — its [nicknames](/docs/organizations/nicknames/) — and your export includes the records filed under **every** spelling of your organization, not only the one on your account.

If a spelling your team uses is not on that list, its records are left out of your exports. That is the most common reason an export looks too small. Email info@puente-dr.org with the spelling and Puente staff will add it.

## Who can export

{/* claim: data-export-members */}
Any member of your organization who can sign in to Manage can press <UI>Export</UI> — you do not have to be an administrator. Puente staff can also download any organization's records.

## Exports contain identified data

{/* claim: data-export-identified */}
A full export contains people's names, ID numbers, phone numbers, birth dates and locations. Treat every file as a patient record.

<Aside type="caution" title="Do not send a full export outside your organization">
Keep exports in a shared folder with named people, not as email attachments, and delete copies you no longer need. To share data with a research partner, turn on the [research file](/docs/data/research-exports/) for that form and send only that.
</Aside>

## FAQ

**Can I export every form at once?**
No. Export each form you need; each one is its own file.

**I pressed Export and nothing downloaded.**
Look for a message in the top corner. If there is none, wait for the button to stop reading Loading….

**The export has fewer records than I expected.**
Check that every phone has sent its forms from Offline Sync, then check that your organization's spellings are all on file. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting/).

**Does exporting change anything in Puente?**
No. It only reads the records.
