Puente helps community health teams collect information about the people they serve, keep it in one place, and use it. Surveyors fill in forms on a phone, in communities with or without signal. Coordinators build those forms, check what arrived and download it, on a computer.

These docs explain how both halves work. Start with the overview below, then follow the path for your role.

## Two apps, one account

{/* claim: start-two-apps */}
{/* claim: start-platforms */}
- **Puente Collect** is the phone app, for [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/puente-collect/id1362371696) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=puente.collect). You use it to find people, fill in forms and send them.
- **Puente Manage** is the website, at [puente-manage.vercel.app](https://puente-manage.vercel.app). You use it to build forms, review and clean records, run your organization and export data.

The same account — your email and password — signs you in to both.

{/* claim: start-languages */}
Both apps work in English, Spanish and Haitian Creole.

## How a survey travels

{/* claim: start-survey-path */}
Every record makes the same trip:

1. **Fill in.** A surveyor opens a form in Collect. The person agrees to the data policy on the consent screen, and the surveyor completes the form.
2. **Wait on the phone.** With no signal, the form is saved on that phone only, and Offline Sync lists it as waiting.
3. **Send.** Back in signal, the surveyor opens Offline Sync — the cloud icon — and taps <UI>Retry</UI>. Nothing is sent until someone does.
4. **Arrive in Manage.** The record is there for everyone in your organization, in the Form Manager and in Data Curation.
5. **Export.** A coordinator downloads the form's records as a spreadsheet.

<Aside type="caution" title="Step 3 is not automatic">
The offline banner says records will sync automatically, but they do not. Until someone taps Retry, the phone holds the only copy. Make it a daily habit. See [Working offline](/docs/collect/offline-sync/).
</Aside>

## Your organization

{/* claim: start-org-scope */}
Everyone in Puente belongs to one organization — the clinic, mission or program they work for. Your organization decides which records you see: your forms, your residents, your exports.

{/* claim: start-org-first-admin */}
The first person to sign up for an organization becomes its **administrator**; everyone after is a **member**. Members can collect and use Manage straight away — there is no approval step. Administrators can also name other administrators and deactivate people who leave. See [Organizations](/docs/organizations/organizations/).

## What you need to begin

{/* claim: start-signin-online */}
- **An account.** One per person, with your own email and password. Never share a login.
- **A phone** with Collect, for anyone who collects data. Signing in needs a connection, and so does sending forms — but filling them in does not.
- **A computer with a web browser**, for anyone who builds forms, reviews records or exports data.
- **Signal at least once a day**, so that the forms collected that day reach Puente the same evening.

## Where to start

**You collect data in the field.** Start with [Start here: data collectors](/docs/get-started/data-collectors/): install Collect, sign in, find people, fill in a form and send it.

**You run things from a computer.** Start with [Start here: data managers](/docs/get-started/data-managers/): set up your organization, build forms, check what arrived and export it.
