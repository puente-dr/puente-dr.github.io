This page is for people who collect data with **Puente Collect** on a phone: promotores, surveyors, clinic volunteers. It covers the few things you need on your first day, in order. Each step links to the full article.

If you coordinate the work from a computer instead, see [Start here: data managers](/docs/get-started/data-managers/).

## Install Collect

{/* claim: start-platforms */}
Install **Puente Collect** from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/puente-collect/id1362371696) on an iPhone (iOS 15.1 or later) or from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=puente.collect) on Android. The first time you open it, choose your language.

Use the phone you will actually carry in the field, so you practise on the screen you will use.

## Sign in

{/* claim: start-signin-online */}
Sign in with your email or phone number and password, then <UI>Log-In</UI>. Signing in **needs an internet connection** — do it before you leave signal, not in the community.

New to Puente? Tap <UI>Sign Up!</UI> and type your organization's name exactly as your team writes it; pick it from the suggestions if it appears. See [Install and sign in](/docs/collect/install-and-sign-in/).

## Learn the five icons

{/* claim: start-tabs */}
The bar at the bottom of Collect has five icons and no words. From left to right:

| Icon | What it opens |
|---|---|
| Magnifying glass | Find people already in Puente |
| Folder | The forms you can fill in |
| House | Home: your activity and your organization's (Collect opens here) |
| Cloud | Offline Sync: forms waiting to be sent |
| Gear | Settings, help and support |

<Shot src={tabs} alt="The Collect tab bar: a magnifying glass, a folder, a house, a cloud with an upward arrow highlighted, and a gear." caption="Five icons, no labels. The highlighted one is the screen you are on." />

See [Getting around Collect](/docs/collect/getting-around/).

## Search before you register

Most people you visit are already in Puente. Before you create anyone, tap the magnifying glass and search — one name at a time: the surname, then the first name, then the nickname. A full name in one search finds nothing. See [Find records](/docs/collect/find-records/).

## Fill in a form, starting with consent

{/* claim: privacy-consent */}
Tap the folder and pick a form. Every form opened there starts on a consent screen: explain the data policy to the person, tick <UI>Community member agrees to data policy</UI> only if they agree, then tap <UI>Continue to Form</UI>.

{/* claim: start-attach-resident */}
<UI>Resident ID</UI> registers a new person. <UI>Vitals</UI>, <UI>Environmental Health</UI> and <UI>Medical Evaluation</UI> are attached to someone already registered — the button reads <UI>Attach a Community Resident</UI> until you choose them. See [Puente forms](/docs/collect/puente-forms/) and [Consent](/docs/collect/consent/).

## Send what you collected

{/* claim: start-survey-path */}
With no signal, a form you finish says <UI>Saved offline — will sync when you reconnect</UI>. It is now only on your phone.

**Sending is not automatic.** When you have signal again — every day — tap the cloud and press <UI>Retry</UI>. You are done when the screen says <UI>No forms queued. You are all caught up!</UI> See [Working offline](/docs/collect/offline-sync/).

## Three things never to do

{/* claim: start-three-nevers */}
1. **Never log out with forms waiting.** Collect will not let you log out offline, and warns you online. Forms left on the phone cannot be sent until you sign back in.
2. **Never delete the app or clear its storage with forms waiting.** Unsent forms live only inside the app on this phone.
3. **Never tap Discard on a form you can still send.** <UI>Discard</UI> deletes it from the phone permanently, and nobody can recover it.

<Aside type="caution" title="If a form will not send">
Try Retry again on a better connection. If it still fails, tell your coordinator before you do anything else.
</Aside>
