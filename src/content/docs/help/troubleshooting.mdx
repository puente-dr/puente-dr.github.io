Most problems in Puente have a short list of causes. Find what you see in the table, then read the section for the detail. If nothing here fits, [contact Puente](/docs/help/contact-support/).

| What you see | Usual cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Collect will not sign you in | No connection, or a wrong email or password | [Signing in](#signing-in) |
| The app is empty after you sign up | Your organization name did not match | [Empty app after sign-up](#empty-app-after-sign-up) |
| A form is missing on the phone | The phone has not refreshed its forms | [A form is not on the phone](#a-form-is-not-on-the-phone) |
| Search finds nobody | A full name typed into a box that reads one field | [Search finds nobody](#search-finds-nobody) |
| Forms still waiting in Offline Sync | Nobody pressed Retry, or the server refused them | [Forms stuck in Offline Sync](#forms-stuck-in-offline-sync) |
| A message instead of an export | Empty form, failed download or expired sign-in | [Export messages](#export-messages) |
| The export has too few records | Forms still on phones, or a spelling of your organization not on file | [Export too small](#export-too-small) |
| You published a form and nothing changed | The publish failed silently | [Publish seemed to do nothing](#publish-seemed-to-do-nothing) |

## Signing in

{/* claim: help-signin-online */}
{/* claim: help-wrong-password */}
{/* claim: help-deactivated */}
- **No signal.** Collect needs an internet connection to sign in; there is no offline sign-in. Connect, then try again.
- **Wrong email, phone or password.** Collect says <UI>Your username or password may be incorrect, please try again.</UI> Check for a stray space, then use <UI>Forgot Password?</UI>: enter your email and Collect sends you a reset link.

## Empty app after sign-up

{/* claim: help-empty-app */}
When you sign up in Collect, the name you type in the organization box must match an organization Puente knows. If it is too close to an existing name — one letter off, say — it is refused, but your account is still created. It is then linked to no organization, so you see none of its forms or activity, and no error.

Do not sign up again. Email info@puente-dr.org with your name, the email you signed up with and the organization you meant. Puente staff will sort it out.

## A form is not on the phone

{/* claim: help-custom-forms-refresh */}
Custom forms reach the phone when you refresh the <UI>Custom Forms</UI> list with the circular arrow beside its heading — not when you sign in. A new phone shows none until someone taps that arrow with a connection. If the list says <UI>No custom forms — try refreshing</UI>, connect and tap that arrow.

Still missing? Check that the form was published for **your** organization, and see [Publish seemed to do nothing](#publish-seemed-to-do-nothing).

## Search finds nobody

{/* claim: help-search */}
The search box matches the **start of one field** — first name, last name, nickname, cédula or household ID. A full name such as `Paciente Ejemplo` finds nothing. Search the surname alone, then the first name, then the nickname.

With no signal, search reads only the residents already saved on the phone. Open Find Records once with a connection before you go out. See [Find records](/docs/collect/find-records/).

## Forms stuck in Offline Sync

{/* claim: help-sync-manual */}
Forms are not sent by themselves, even though the offline banner says they will sync automatically. Nothing leaves the phone until someone opens Offline Sync — the cloud — and taps <UI>Retry</UI>.

{/* claim: help-sync-failures */}
If Retry does not empty the list, read the message:

- <UI>You are offline. Reconnect to the internet and try again.</UI> — the phone never reached Puente. Find a better connection.
- <UI>The server did not accept these forms. Try again in a few minutes, and contact support if it keeps happening.</UI> — wait and retry; if the same forms keep failing on a good connection, [contact Puente](/docs/help/contact-support/).

Either way, <UI>Your forms are still saved on this device. Nothing has been lost.</UI>

<Shot phone src={queue} alt="The Offline Sync screen reading 1 form is saved on this device and has not reached the server yet, with one Resident ID form listed under Waiting to sync, a Discard link and a Retry button." caption="Retry sends what is waiting. {/* claim: help-household-queue */}
A household created offline is not listed here, so the screen can say <UI>No forms queued. You are all caught up!</UI> while a household still waits. It is sent on the next Retry, so press Retry once more after you reconnect.

<Aside type="caution" title="Do not discard a form to make the list go away">
<UI>Discard</UI> deletes the form from the phone permanently and it cannot be recovered. Ask your coordinator first.
</Aside>

{/* claim: help-logout-blocked */}
Do not log out to fix a stuck form: Collect will not let you log out while offline, and forms waiting on the phone cannot be sent until you sign back in.

## Export messages

{/* claim: data-export-messages */}
<UI>This form has no records to export yet.</UI> means nothing has reached Puente on that form. <UI>The export could not be completed. Please try again.</UI> means the download broke; try again. <UI>Your sign-in has expired. Sign in again to export.</UI> means sign in again first. See [Exporting data](/docs/data/exporting-data/).

## Export too small

{/* claim: help-export-small */}
1. Check every phone: forms still in Offline Sync are not in Puente yet.
2. Check the spelling. An export includes your organization's name and its nicknames. Records filed under a spelling that is not on that list are left out. Only Puente staff can add a nickname: email info@puente-dr.org with the spelling.

## Publish seemed to do nothing

{/* claim: help-publish-silent */}
When <UI>Publish</UI> fails in the Form Creator, Manage does not show an error. So after publishing, always check: open <UI>Manage</UI> to see the form in the list, then refresh <UI>Custom Forms</UI> on a phone and open it. If it is not there, publish again; if it still does not appear, [contact Puente](/docs/help/contact-support/) with the form's name.
