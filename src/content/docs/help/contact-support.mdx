Most day-to-day problems can be solved inside your own team: an administrator can add or remove people, and [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting/) covers the common errors. Some changes, though, only Puente staff can make. This page lists them and explains how to write to Puente so you get an answer quickly.

## How to reach Puente

Email [info@puente-dr.org](mailto:info@puente-dr.org).

{/* claim: help-feedback-email */}
From the phone: tap the gear, then <UI>Support</UI>, then <UI>Contact us / Feedback</UI>. Type your message and tap <UI>Send Mail</UI>; Collect opens your email app with a message to info@puente-dr.org, ready to send.

## What only Puente can do

{/* claim: help-staff-add-org */}
{/* claim: help-staff-aliases */}
{/* claim: help-staff-org-change */}
{/* claim: help-empty-app */}
{/* claim: help-staff-last-admin */}
{/* claim: help-staff-blocks */}
{/* claim: data-research-study-id */}
- **Add your organization to the Manage sign-up list.** The web sign-up only lists organizations already on file. Use <UI>My organization isn't listed</UI>, which opens an email to Puente, and do not pick a close match instead.
- **Add a nickname for your organization** — another spelling your team uses. The Organizations screen shows a <UI>Save spellings</UI> box, but only Puente staff can save it. See [Nicknames](/docs/organizations/nicknames/).
- **Move an account to another organization.** Your organization decides which records you can see, so you cannot change it yourself.
- **Sort out a refused organization name.** If Collect refused the name you typed at sign-up, your account exists but is not linked to an organization.
- **Replace the last administrator.** An organization's last administrator cannot be demoted or deactivated. If that person has left, Puente can appoint someone else.
- **Ask for a new kind of question.** The Form Creator offers seven blocks. If your program needs something they cannot do, tell Puente what and why.
- **Look up a study ID.** Only Puente can turn a research file's `P-…` code back into a person.
- **Delete an account or personal data.** See [Delete your account](/docs/privacy/delete-your-account/).

## What to include

A message Puente can act on right away usually has:

- your **name** and your **organization**, spelled the way you signed up;
- the **app** — Collect or Manage — and, for Collect, the phone type and app version;
- **what you were doing**, step by step, and **what you expected**;
- the **exact message** on screen, copied word for word;
- the **form's name** and roughly **when** it happened;
- for a nickname, the **exact spelling** your team uses.

{/* claim: help-version-label */}
The Collect version number is shown on the gear's settings screen, after the word Version.

## What never to send

<Aside type="caution" title="Keep patient data out of email">
Never send passwords, patient names, cédula numbers, phone numbers, health details, exported files, or screenshots that show any of these. Describe the record instead: the form, the community and the date. If Puente needs more, it will ask.
</Aside>

For a study ID lookup, send the code only. Never send a code and a name together.

## Urgent or can wait

Write **"Urgent"** at the start of the subject when:

{/* claim: help-sync-failures */}
- forms will not leave a phone after several tries on a good connection and the app says the server did not accept them — the phone holds the only copy;
- someone who left still has access (today <UI>Deactivate</UI> signs people out but does not stop them signing in again);
- exports keep failing before a deadline.

These **can wait** for a normal reply: new nicknames, adding an organization to the sign-up list, moving an account, and requests for new question types.

## FAQ

{/* claim: privacy-deactivate */}
**Can my administrator do any of this instead?**
An administrator can make someone an administrator, deactivate and reactivate members of your own organization. Everything listed above needs Puente.
