{/* claim: manage-one-account */}
Puente Manage is the web app where coordinators review what the field sent in, build forms and export data. It runs in any browser at [puente-manage.vercel.app](https://puente-manage.vercel.app). You use the same account as in Puente Collect, the phone app.

This page covers the sign-in screen, creating an account, and resetting a forgotten password.

## Sign in

{/* claim: manage-signin-page */}
Open Manage. The screen is headed **Puente Manage**, with two boxes: <UI>Email or phone number</UI> and <UI>Password</UI>. Type the email you registered with and your password, then press <UI>Sign in →</UI> (or Enter).

{/* claim: manage-signin-dashboard */}
Manage opens on <UI>Review</UI>, the dashboard. If you followed a link to another page while signed out, it takes you there instead.

<Shot src={login} alt="The Manage sign-in page: Email or phone number and Password boxes, a blue Sign in button, Forgot password and Create account links, a yellow note about Collect, and a language row under it." caption="The sign-in page. The language row at the bottom works before you sign in." />

{/* claim: manage-collect-hint */}
The yellow note reads <UI>Collecting in the field? Open Puente Collect on your phone instead.</UI> Manage is for the office. Surveys are filled in on the phone, in [Collect](/docs/collect/install-and-sign-in/).

{/* claim: manage-signin-language */}
To change the language before you sign in, use the row at the bottom of the card: **English**, **Español** or **Kreyòl Ayisyen**.

## Create an account

Press <UI>Create account</UI> on the sign-in page.

{/* claim: manage-register-fields */}
| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| <UI>First Name</UI>, <UI>Last Name</UI> | No, but fill them in so your team can tell who you are |
| <UI>Organization</UI> | Yes |
| <UI>Email Address</UI> | Yes |
| <UI>Phone Number</UI> | No |
| <UI>Password</UI>, <UI>Confirm Password</UI> | Yes, and the two must match |

<Shot src={register} alt="The Create an account form: First Name, Last Name, an Organization picker reading Please Select, a My organization isn't listed link, Email Address, Phone Number, Password, Confirm Password, and Register and Cancel buttons." caption="Required fields are marked with an asterisk." />

{/* claim: manage-register-email-confirm */}
Press <UI>Register</UI>. Manage sends a confirmation to your email and opens the dashboard.

{/* claim: org-first-admin */}
The first person to register in an organization becomes its administrator; everyone after is a member. See [Members and administrators](/docs/organizations/members-and-administrators/).

## Choose your organization

{/* claim: manage-register-org-list */}
<UI>Organization</UI> is a list of the organizations already in Puente. Type a few letters to narrow it, then pick yours. If the list could not load, the form says <UI>We could not load the list of organizations. Check your connection and reload the page.</UI>

<Shot src={picker} alt="The Organization field with Exam typed in it and a single suggestion, Example Health Trust, below." caption="Typing narrows the list. Pick the exact name, not a close one." />

<Aside type="caution" title="Do not pick an organization that is nearly right">
Your organization decides which records you can see and where your work is filed. A close-enough choice puts you in someone else's group. You cannot change it yourself afterwards.
</Aside>

## When your organization isn't listed

{/* claim: manage-register-not-listed */}
Press <UI>My organization isn't listed</UI>. Manage explains that Puente staff add organizations by hand, and offers a link, <UI>Email us your organization's name</UI>. It opens an email to info@puente-dr.org with the subject "Add my organization to Puente Manage". Once your organization appears in the list, come back and finish registering.

<Shot src={notListed} alt="Close-up of the registration form after pressing My organization isn't listed: a paragraph saying Puente staff add organizations by hand, ending in the link Email us your organization's name." caption="The help text appears right under the Organization field." />

{/* claim: org-collect-creates */}
Collect works differently: there you can type a new organization name and Puente creates it. [Organizations](/docs/organizations/organizations/) explains both ways in.

## Reset a forgotten password

{/* claim: manage-forgot-password */}
1. Press <UI>Forgot password</UI> on the sign-in page. The screen reads <UI>Reset your account</UI>.
2. Type your email in <UI>Phone Number or Email Address</UI>.
3. Leave <UI>Send account reset email?</UI> selected and press <UI>Send reset email</UI>.
4. When <UI>Request Sent!</UI> appears, open the email and follow the link.

<Aside type="tip">
Use the email option. The screen also offers a text message, but Puente no longer sends texts for account confirmation, and the text option has not been confirmed to work.
</Aside>

## FAQ

**Do I need a separate account for Manage?**
No. The account you use in Collect works here, and the reverse.

{/* claim: org-refused-effect */}
**I signed in and the dashboard is empty. Why?**
Usually your organization does not match one Puente knows, or nothing has synced yet. See [Organizations](/docs/organizations/organizations/).

{/* claim: manage-as-org-readonly */}
**Can I change my organization later?**
Not yourself. [Account settings](/docs/manage/account-settings/) shows it read-only; email info@puente-dr.org to change it.
