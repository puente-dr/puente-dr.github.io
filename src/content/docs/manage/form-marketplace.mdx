The Form Marketplace is a shelf of ready-made forms that every organization can see. Instead of building a household water survey from nothing, you copy one from the Marketplace, change what you need, and publish it as your own custom form.

{/* claim: manage-mp-duplicate */}
Duplicating never changes the Marketplace original: you always work on your own copy. Before you publish that copy, set its type (see [Duplicate a form](#duplicate-a-form)), or other organizations will see it.

## Open the Marketplace

{/* claim: manage-mp-open */}
Press <UI>Market</UI> in the left menu. The page is titled <UI>Form Marketplace</UI>, with the line <UI>Browse and duplicate community data collection forms.</UI>

## Browse the forms

{/* claim: manage-mp-cards */}
Each form is a card with its name, a short description of what it collects, and a <UI>Duplicate</UI> link.

<Shot src={market} alt="The Form Marketplace with three cards: Household water survey, Child growth check and Clinic sign-in sheet, each with a one-line description and a Duplicate link." caption="Read the description to see what each form is for. The forms shown here are examples." />

The cards show only the name and description. To see the questions inside a form, duplicate it: the copy opens in the Form Creator, where you can read every block and press <UI>Preview form</UI> to see it as the phone will.

{/* claim: manage-mp-empty */}
If there is nothing on the shelf, the page reads <UI>No forms available yet.</UI>

## Duplicate a form

{/* claim: manage-mp-duplicate */}
1. Press <UI>Duplicate</UI> on the card.
2. The [Form Creator](/docs/manage/form-creator/) opens with the form's name, description and questions already filled in.
3. Change what you need: rename the form for your project, reword questions, add or remove blocks. To add a question, paste its full wording into the new block in one go (see [Form Creator](/docs/manage/form-creator/)).
4. Set <UI>Type of form</UI> to <UI>Assets</UI>, then back to <UI>Custom</UI>.
5. Press <UI>Publish</UI>. The form is saved as a new form belonging to your organization.

<Shot src={loaded} alt="The Form Creator with a form loaded: its name and description filled in, the Allow research export checkbox, and a Single select question about water source in the Form builder." caption="A duplicated form opens like this, ready to change before you publish." />

The Marketplace form itself is not changed.

{/* claim: manage-mp-copy-type */}
<Aside type="danger" title="Step 4 keeps your copy private">
A copy keeps the Marketplace form's type, even though <UI>Type of form</UI> already shows <UI>Custom</UI>. Published without step 4, the copy appears on the Marketplace for every organization, and it may not reach your own phones.
</Aside>

{/* claim: manage-fc-publish-silent */}
<Aside type="caution" title="Check the copy before your team uses it">
A duplicated form is only on phones once it is published. As with any form, a failed publish shows no error. After you publish, find the form in the [Form Manager](/docs/manage/form-manager/), then refresh Custom Forms on a phone and fill it in once yourself.
</Aside>

## Adapt it well

{/* claim: manage-mp-research-copy */}
A ready-made form is a starting point, not a finished survey. Before you publish:

- **Rename it.** Give the copy a name your surveyors will recognise, such as the project and community. It is also the name of the export file.
- **Cut what you will not use.** Every question costs time at a doorstep.
- **Use the number block for numbers.** It gives surveyors a number keypad.
- **Decide on research export.** The copy keeps the original's <UI>Allow research export</UI> setting. Leave it ticked only if a research partner with ethics approval will receive the de-identified file.

## Where Marketplace forms come from

{/* claim: manage-mp-source */}
The Marketplace lists every form whose type is Marketplace, whichever organization saved it. Every organization sees the same shelf. The Form Creator's <UI>Type of form</UI> offers only <UI>Custom</UI> and <UI>Assets</UI>, but a copy published without changing its type lands on the shelf too. If your organization built a form others could use, email info@puente-dr.org.

## FAQ

{/* claim: manage-mp-source */}
**Can we copy a form from another organization?**
Only if it is in the Marketplace. Other organizations' custom forms are not shown to you.

**Does duplicating send the form to our phones?**
No. It opens a copy in the Form Creator. Phones get it only after you publish.

{/* claim: manage-fc-stale */}
**Can I see the questions before duplicating?**
Not on the card. Duplicating opens the copy in the Form Creator, and nothing is saved until you publish. When you are done looking, reload the page: until you do, the copy comes back every time you open <UI>Create</UI>.

{/* claim: manage-fc-fix-type */}
**Can other organizations see the form we publish?**
Not if you did step 4: then it appears only in your Form Manager and on your team's phones. If you published without it, open the form with <UI>Edit</UI> in the Form Manager, set <UI>Type of form</UI> to <UI>Assets</UI> and back to <UI>Custom</UI>, and publish again.

**I changed my mind after duplicating. Do I need to delete something?**
No. Nothing is saved until you publish. Reload the page so the copy does not come back the next time you open Create.
