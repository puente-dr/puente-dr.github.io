The Form Manager lists every form your organization collects with. It is where you start an export, open a form to edit it, copy one as the start of a new form, or retire one you no longer use.

## Open the Form Manager

{/* claim: manage-fm-open */}
In the left menu, press <UI>Manage</UI>. The page is titled <UI>Form Manager</UI>. It has two lists: <UI>Puente Forms</UI> at the top and your <UI>Custom forms</UI> below.

<Shot src={manager} alt="The Form Manager: a Create form button and a Search forms box, a Puente Forms table listing SurveyData, HistoryEnvironmentalHealth, Vitals and EvaluationMedical with an Export button each, and a Custom Forms table whose rows have Edit, Duplicate, Delete and Export." caption="Puente's forms have only Export. Your own forms can also be edited, copied and deleted." />

{/* claim: manage-fm-search-create */}
<UI>+ Create form</UI> opens an empty [Form Creator](/docs/manage/form-creator/). <UI>Search forms…</UI> narrows both lists to forms whose name contains what you type.

## Puente's forms and their names in Collect

{/* claim: manage-fm-puente-forms */}
Manage shows Puente's four built-in forms by their internal names. On the phone they have friendlier names:

| In Manage | In Collect |
|---|---|
| SurveyData | Resident ID |
| HistoryEnvironmentalHealth | Environmental Health |
| Vitals | Vitals |
| EvaluationMedical | Medical Evaluation |

SurveyData holds the people themselves — one row per resident. The other three are visits attached to a resident. See [Puente forms](/docs/collect/puente-forms/).

{/* claim: manage-fm-custom-actions */}
These four can only be exported. You cannot edit, copy or delete them here.

## Custom forms and their buttons

Custom forms are the ones your organization built. Each row has four buttons:

{/* claim: manage-fm-edit-dup */}
- <UI>Edit</UI> opens the form in the Form Creator. Publishing saves your changes to the same form.
- <UI>Duplicate</UI> opens a copy in the Form Creator. Publishing saves it as a new form; the original is untouched.
- <UI>Delete</UI> retires the form (see below).
- <UI>Export</UI> downloads its answers as a spreadsheet.

{/* claim: manage-fm-records-view */}
Press a form's name to see its records in a table. <UI>← Back</UI> returns to the list.

## See how many forms were collected

{/* claim: manage-fm-expand */}
Press the arrow at the start of a row to open it. The row shows <UI>Forms collected</UI> with a number, and a <UI>Refresh</UI> button.

<Aside type="caution" title="The count starts at 0">
The number reads 0 until you press <UI>Refresh</UI> — it is not fetched when the row opens. Press <UI>Refresh</UI> before you read it. If the count fails to load, the row says <UI>These records did not load.</UI> instead of showing a false zero.
</Aside>

## Research forms: two export buttons

{/* claim: manage-fm-research */}
A custom form built with <UI>Allow research export</UI> ticked shows the badge <UI>Research export on</UI> under its name, and two buttons in place of <UI>Export</UI>:

- <UI>Export research file</UI> — a study ID, age, sex, community and the form's answers. No names, ID numbers, phone numbers, birth dates or locations. This is the only file to send to a research partner.
- <UI>Export full records</UI> — every answer with the person's name, ID number, phone number, birth date and location. Keep it inside Puente.

The expanded row repeats both descriptions. Puente's four built-in forms never have a research file. See [Research exports](/docs/data/research-exports/).

## Export a form

{/* claim: manage-fm-export-csv */}
Press <UI>Export</UI>. Your browser downloads a CSV file named after the form. A research file has "research" and the date in its name, so the two files are hard to mix up. The file opens with accents intact in Excel.

{/* claim: org-member-exports */}
Any member of your organization can download the full, identified records — not only administrators. Treat every export as private. [Exporting data](/docs/data/exporting-data/) explains what each file contains.

{/* claim: manage-fm-export-messages */}
If nothing downloads, a message in the top corner says why:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| <UI>This form has no records to export yet.</UI> | Nothing has synced for this form. Check the phones. |
| <UI>The export could not be completed. Please try again.</UI> | Try again. The download failed; your records are not missing. |
| <UI>Your sign-in has expired. Sign in again to export.</UI> | Sign out, sign back in, and export again. |

## Delete a form

{/* claim: manage-fm-delete */}
Press <UI>Delete</UI>, then confirm <UI>Delete form</UI> when asked <UI>Do you want to remove this form?</UI> The form leaves the Form Manager, and phones stop listing it the next time they refresh their custom forms. Its answers are not erased — delete marks the form retired.

## FAQ

**Why does a form I built not appear?**
If the list could not load, the page says <UI>Your custom forms did not load.</UI> with a <UI>Try again</UI> button. Otherwise, check that the Form Creator really published it.

**Why can't I edit SurveyData?**
Puente's four built-in forms have only an Export button. Edit, Duplicate and Delete belong to custom forms.

**Who can export full records?**
Any member of your organization.
