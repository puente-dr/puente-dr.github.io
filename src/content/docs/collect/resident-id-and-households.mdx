**Resident ID** is the form that creates a person in Puente. Every other Puente form — Environmental Health, Medical Evaluation, Vitals — attaches to a person, so the person has to exist first. You fill in Resident ID once per person, the first time you meet them.

Before you start one, search for the person under the magnifying glass. Most people you visit are already registered; see [Find records](/docs/collect/find-records/).

## Start a Resident ID

{/* claim: collect-rid-start */}
Tap the folder, then the <UI>Resident ID</UI> card under <UI>Puente Forms</UI>. The form opens on the consent screen; once the person agrees and you continue, the form itself appears. See [Consent](/docs/collect/consent/).

## What the form records

{/* claim: collect-rid-fields */}
The form has three parts.

**Demographics**

| Field | Required? |
|---|---|
| <UI>First Name</UI>, <UI>Last Name</UI> | Required |
| <UI>Nickname</UI> | Optional — the name people actually use. Search reads it. |
| <UI>Date of Birth</UI> (day, month, year) | Optional |
| <UI>Age (if their date of birth isn't available)</UI> | Optional |
| <UI>Sex</UI> | Required |
| <UI>Telephone Number</UI> | Optional |
| <UI>Marriage Status</UI> | Required |
| <UI>Occupation</UI> | Optional |
| <UI>Education Level</UI> | Required |

**Location** — <UI>Community Name</UI>, <UI>Sub-county</UI>, <UI>City</UI>, <UI>Province</UI>, <UI>Region</UI> and <UI>Country</UI>, all optional. These fields suggest names already recorded in Puente as you type; pick a suggestion so the same community is not spelled three ways. Then the map point and a photo (below).

## Save the form

{/* claim: collect-rid-save */}
The button at the bottom reads <UI>Empty Form</UI> until you type something, then <UI>Save Record</UI>. If a required field is empty, the form tells you which one and does not save.

When you save, the short message that pops up tells you where the record is:

- <UI>Form successfully submitted</UI>, after the person's name — it reached Puente.
- <UI>Saved offline — will sync when you reconnect</UI> — it is on the phone only, waiting for you to send it from the cloud. See [Offline and sync](/docs/collect/offline-sync/).
- <UI>ERROR!</UI> — the record was not saved. Save it again.

Read that short message: the page behind it says <UI>Form successfully submitted</UI> either way.

{/* claim: collect-suggested-next */}
After a Resident ID saves, the screen shows <UI>Suggested next forms</UI>: your pinned forms. Tap one and it opens for the person you just registered, so you do not have to search for them. A pinned Resident ID starts a new person instead.

<Aside type="caution" title="Suggested next forms do not ask again">
A form opened from <UI>Suggested next forms</UI>, right after a Resident ID, does not show the consent screen: the person you just registered gave consent a moment ago, at the start of the Resident ID. If you switch to a different person in that form, go back to the folder and open the form from there instead, so the new person gets their own consent screen.
</Aside>

## Record the location

{/* claim: collect-rid-location */}
Location is **not** recorded automatically. Stand at the home and tap <UI>Get Location Again</UI>; the coordinates appear under the button. Until you tap it, the form shows (0.00, 0.00).

If the phone cannot find its position within 20 seconds, the app shows a timeout message and the form will save 0, 0 — a point off the coast of Africa — even if numbers from an earlier tap are still on screen. Step outside, wait a moment, and tap the button again until it works with no timeout message.

<Aside type="caution" title="0.00, 0.00 is not a location">
A record saved with (0.00, 0.00) has no usable map point. If your last tap ended in a timeout message, tap again before you save.
</Aside>

## Add a photo

{/* claim: collect-rid-photo */}
Under <UI>Photo Assessment</UI>, tap <UI>Take Photo</UI> to use the camera, or <UI>Use image from camera roll</UI> to choose one already on the phone. The photo is optional. Ask the person first.

## Link the person to a household

{/* claim: collect-household-options */}
At the end of the form, under <UI>Household</UI>, choose one of three options:

The coordinates still read 0.00 because the button has not been tapped." />
</div>

{/* claim: collect-household-nothing */}
- <UI>Do Nothing</UI> — no household. The form says <UI>You have chosen not to link a household.</UI> and offers <UI>Add or Create a Household</UI> if you change your mind.

{/* claim: collect-household-create */}
- <UI>Create a new household</UI> — for the first person you register in a home. Tap the <UI>Household</UI> button that appears; the form shows <UI>You have successfully created a new household.</UI> straight away. If an <UI>ERROR!</UI> message also appears, the household was not created: tap the button again.

{/* claim: collect-household-link */}
- <UI>Link this individual to an existing house</UI> — for everyone else in that home. A search opens: find someone already linked to that household, then choose this person's role (parent, sibling, grandparent, cousin or other) and submit. The form shows <UI>Linked</UI>; if an <UI>ERROR!</UI> message also appears, the link was not saved.

Linking saves this person's role, but in this version it does not give them the same household ID as the person you chose. If your program needs to know who lives together, agree with your coordinator how to record it.

## FAQ

{/* claim: collect-rid-fields */}
**Which fields must I fill in?**
First name, last name, sex, marriage status and education level. Everything else is optional.

{/* claim: collect-households-not-listed */}
**I created a household offline. Why is it not in Offline Sync?**
Households saved offline are not listed there. They are sent only together with other waiting forms, so if a household is the only thing you saved offline, keep the phone as it is and tell your coordinator. See [Offline and sync](/docs/collect/offline-sync/).

{/* claim: collect-duplicates */}
**I registered someone twice. What now?**
Tell your coordinator both names and the date. Someone with Manage access can find the duplicate and delete it in [Data Curation](/docs/manage/data-curation/).
