Custom forms are the questions your organization writes for itself — a water survey, a vision exam, a follow-up visit. Someone builds them in Puente Manage, and they appear in Collect for everyone in the organization, beside the four Puente forms.

This page covers the Collect side: finding the forms, keeping the list current, pinning, and filling them in. To build one, see [Form Creator](/docs/manage/form-creator/).

## Where custom forms come from

{/* claim: collect-custom-source */}
A custom form starts in Manage's Form Creator, with <UI>Type of form</UI> set to <UI>Custom</UI>, and reaches phones when someone presses <UI>Publish</UI>. Collect then lists every active custom form for your organization, including forms tagged with any other spelling your organization goes by.

Publish, at the top right, sends it to phones." />

{/* claim: collect-publish-silent */}
<Aside type="caution" title="Check the phone after publishing">
Manage does not tell you when a publish fails. After you publish, refresh Custom Forms on a phone and make sure the form is there before anyone goes into the field.
</Aside>

## Find your organization's forms

{/* claim: collect-forms-tab */}
Tap the folder. Your organization's forms are under <UI>Custom Forms</UI>, below <UI>Puente Forms</UI> and <UI>Pinned Forms</UI>. Swipe the row sideways if there are more than fit on the screen.

## Refresh the list

{/* claim: collect-custom-refresh */}
The phone keeps a saved copy of your organization's custom forms, so they keep working with no signal. To fetch new ones, tap the round arrow beside <UI>Custom Forms</UI> while you have a connection. Do it on a new phone, after signing in, and whenever someone publishes a form. (<UI>Download records for offline use</UI>, under the gear in <UI>Offline Records</UI>, fetches them too.)

If the list is empty, the card reads <UI>No custom forms — try refreshing</UI>. Refreshing without a connection keeps the list the phone already has.

<Aside type="tip" title="Refresh before you leave">
Make refreshing part of packing up for a field day. A form published after you left signal cannot reach your phone until you are back.
</Aside>

## Fill in a custom form

{/* claim: collect-custom-attach */}
Tap the form. Like every form opened from the folder, it opens on the consent screen; see [Consent](/docs/collect/consent/). Then it works like Vitals: a <UI>Search by name or ID</UI> box sits above the questions, and the button at the bottom reads <UI>Attach a Community Resident</UI> until you choose the person the answers are about. Once someone is attached, it reads <UI>Save Record</UI>.

{/* claim: collect-supp-saved */}
Saving shows the same messages as the Puente forms. <UI>Saved offline — will sync when you reconnect</UI> means it is waiting on the phone. <UI>Form successfully submitted</UI> usually means it reached Puente, but a form saved with no signal for someone already in Puente shows it too, so check the cloud for anything waiting. <UI>ERROR!</UI> means it was not saved. A custom form waiting in Offline Sync is listed by its own title.

## Pin the forms you use most

{/* claim: collect-pin */}
Press and hold any form — a custom form or one of the Puente forms — to pin it. It appears under <UI>Pinned Forms</UI>, just below Puente Forms. To unpin, press and hold it under <UI>Pinned Forms</UI>.

With nothing pinned, the section reads <UI>No pinned forms yet — long-press any form to pin it</UI>. Pins are kept on this phone only.

{/* claim: collect-suggested-next */}
Pinned forms do a second job. After you save a Resident ID, the <UI>Suggested next forms</UI> screen lists them; tapping one opens it for the person you just registered. Pin the forms you fill in after every registration and you skip the search each time.

<Aside type="caution" title="Suggested next forms do not ask again">
A form opened from <UI>Suggested next forms</UI>, right after a Resident ID, does not show the consent screen: the person you just registered gave consent a moment ago, at the start of the Resident ID. If you switch to a different person in that form, go back to the folder and open the form from there instead, so the new person gets their own consent screen.
</Aside>

## Workflows

{/* claim: collect-workflows */}
Below <UI>Custom Forms</UI>, the <UI>Workflows</UI> section lists the same custom forms grouped by workflow. Its refresh arrow fetches the custom forms; the groups catch up the next time you open the folder. Forms published from today's Form Creator carry no workflow, so they all appear in one group, <UI>No Workflow Assigned</UI>.

## Records and exports

{/* claim: collect-custom-manage */}
Everything collected with a custom form shows up in Manage under that form's row in <UI>Form Manager</UI>, where it can be exported.

## FAQ

{/* claim: collect-custom-refresh */}
**A colleague can see a new form and I can't. Why?**
Your phone has the list from the last time it fetched forms. Tap the arrow beside Custom Forms with a connection.

{/* claim: collect-custom-attach */}
**Why can't I save the form?**
No resident is attached. Search for the person at the top of the form and tap their name in the list.

{/* claim: collect-pin-cleared */}
**My pinned forms disappeared.**
Pins live on the phone. Tapping <UI>Delete Credentials</UI> on the sign-in screen clears them, and so does a new phone. Pin them again.
