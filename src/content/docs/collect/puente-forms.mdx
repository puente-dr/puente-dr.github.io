Every organization's copy of Collect comes with the same four forms, listed under <UI>Puente Forms</UI> when you tap the folder. One registers a person; the other three add to that person's record. Your organization can add its own forms beside them; see [Custom forms](/docs/collect/custom-forms/).

## The four forms

{/* claim: collect-builtin-forms */}
| Form in Collect | What it is for | Name in Manage and exports |
|---|---|---|
| <UI>Resident ID</UI> | Registers a person: who they are, where they live, their household. | SurveyData |
| <UI>Environmental Health</UI> | The home and its surroundings. | HistoryEnvironmentalHealth |
| <UI>Medical Evaluation</UI> | A health complaint and the plan for it. | EvaluationMedical |
| <UI>Vitals</UI> | Measurements: height, weight, blood pressure and more. | Vitals |

Manage lists these forms by their internal names, shown in the last column. Knowing both names saves confusion when you look for your records in [Form Manager](/docs/manage/form-manager/).

## Resident ID

Resident ID creates the person. Fill it in once, the first time you meet someone, and use the other three forms on every visit after. [Resident ID and households](/docs/collect/resident-id-and-households/) covers every field.

## Environmental Health

{/* claim: collect-env-fields */}
Questions about the household's living conditions:

- How long the person has lived in the community and in this house, and the community's biggest problem.
- Water: how often they have access, and what kind they drink.
- Bathroom, latrine, clinic and dental access, and how often trash is collected.
- The house itself: ownership, floor and roof condition, what the floor and the house are made of, type of stove, and access to electricity.
- Whether the family worried about having enough food in the past month, and any government assistance.
- How many people live in the house, and how many children by age group.

The number of people living in the house is required; the rest is optional.

## Medical Evaluation

{/* claim: collect-med-fields */}
A clinical note about one health problem:

- Chronic conditions: hypertension, diabetes, other.
- Whether they have seen a doctor, and the status of their care.
- The part of the body affected, how long it has lasted, whether it began suddenly or gradually, and whether it is getting better or worse.
- The evaluator's assessment: whether a general consult or surgery is needed, whether immediate follow-up is required, and the plan.

No field is required.

## Vitals

{/* claim: collect-vitals-fields */}
Measurements: <UI>Height</UI> (inches), <UI>Weight</UI> (pounds), <UI>Body Mass Index</UI>, <UI>Body Temperature</UI> (°Celsius), <UI>Pulse</UI>, <UI>Respiratory Rate</UI>, <UI>Blood Pressure</UI> (systolic over diastolic), <UI>Blood Oxygen</UI>, fasting blood glucose, hemoglobin, hemoglobin A1c, and <UI>Pain</UI>. No field is required, so record what you measured and leave the rest blank.

Height is in inches and weight in pounds. If you measured in centimeters or kilograms, convert before you type.

## Attach the form to a resident

{/* claim: collect-attach-resident */}
Environmental Health, Medical Evaluation and Vitals all belong to a person. Each opens with a <UI>Search by name or ID</UI> box above the questions. Search for the person, tap their name in the list, then fill in the form.

Until you choose someone, the button at the bottom reads <UI>Attach a Community Resident</UI> and does nothing. Once a resident is attached it changes to <UI>Save Record</UI>.

There are quicker ways in that skip the search:

{/* claim: collect-suggested-next */}
- **Right after a Resident ID.** The <UI>Suggested next forms</UI> screen shows your pinned forms; tap one and it opens for the person you just registered. A pinned Resident ID starts a new person instead.

<Aside type="caution" title="Suggested next forms do not ask again">
A form opened from <UI>Suggested next forms</UI>, right after a Resident ID, does not show the consent screen: the person you just registered gave consent a moment ago, at the start of the Resident ID. If you switch to a different person in that form, go back to the folder and open the form from there instead, so the new person gets their own consent screen.
</Aside>
- **From the person's record.** Open them under the magnifying glass and start a form from there. See [Find records](/docs/collect/find-records/).

## When you save

{/* claim: collect-supp-saved */}
<UI>Saved offline — will sync when you reconnect</UI> means the form is waiting on the phone. <UI>Form successfully submitted</UI> usually means it reached Puente, but a form saved with no signal for someone already in Puente shows it too, so check the cloud for anything waiting. <UI>ERROR!</UI> means the form was not saved. Send waiting forms from the cloud; see [Offline and sync](/docs/collect/offline-sync/).

## FAQ

{/* claim: collect-attach-resident */}
**Why can't I save Vitals?**
No resident is attached. Search for the person at the top of the form and tap their name in the list.

{/* claim: collect-builtin-fixed */}
**Can I change the questions on these forms?**
No. The four Puente forms are the same for every organization. To ask your own questions, build a custom form in Manage; see [Form Creator](/docs/manage/form-creator/).

**The person isn't found when I search. What do I do?**
Search by surname alone, then nickname. If they really are new, register them with Resident ID first.
