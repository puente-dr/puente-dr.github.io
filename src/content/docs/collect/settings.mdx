The gear, the last icon at the bottom of Collect, opens **Account Settings**. It holds your account details, how the app looks and behaves on this phone, the help options, and the log-out button.

The screen has two tabs at the top: <UI>Settings</UI> and <UI>Support</UI>.

## The Settings tab

{/* claim: collect-settings-list */}
From top to bottom:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| <UI>Name, Phone, Email</UI> | Edit your first and last name, phone number and email. |
| <UI>Change Password</UI> | Set a new password. You type your current one first. |
| <UI>Find Records</UI> | How many residents the phone keeps for offline search. |
| <UI>Language</UI> | Switch the app between English, Spanish and Haitian Creole. |
| <UI>Offline Records</UI> | Download resident records for offline search, or clear them. |
| <UI>Theme</UI> | <UI>Light</UI> or <UI>Dark</UI>. |
| <UI>Calm Mode</UI> | <UI>Reduce animations for clinical focus</UI>. |
| <UI>Reset Onboarding</UI> | Show the first-launch tour and tips again. |

## Your name, phone, email and password

{/* claim: collect-settings-online-only */}
Changes to your account need a connection. Offline, the app says <UI>You are offline. This change needs an internet connection, and has not been saved.</UI> A new password must be at least 8 characters, typed twice. Your organization is not on this screen; to change it, email info@puente-dr.org.

## Language

{/* claim: collect-language-settings */}
<UI>Language</UI> opens <UI>Choose Language</UI> with three buttons: <UI>English</UI>, <UI>Spanish</UI> and <UI>Haitian Creole</UI>. The whole app switches, including the consent screen.

## Records kept for offline search

{/* claim: collect-settings-storage-limit */}
<UI>Find Records</UI> shows how many residents are stored on the phone and the <UI>Record storage limit</UI>, which starts at 2,000. Raise it before you travel to a community with more residents than that. [Find records](/docs/collect/find-records/) explains the search.

<div class="shots">
<Shot phone src={storage} alt="The Find Records settings screen showing Current records stored 210 and Record storage limit 2000 with an Edit link." caption="Current records stored is how many people you can search offline right now." />
</div>

{/* claim: collect-offline-records */}
<UI>Offline Records</UI> has two buttons:

- <UI>Download records for offline use</UI> — fetches resident records now, while you have signal.
- <UI>Clear downloaded records</UI> — deletes the residents saved for search. The app asks first and says offline search will be empty until you download again.

Clearing downloaded records does not touch forms waiting in Offline Sync. Those are a separate list.

## Theme and Calm Mode

{/* claim: collect-theme-calm */}
<UI>Theme</UI> switches between <UI>Light</UI> and <UI>Dark</UI>. <UI>Calm Mode</UI> turns down the app's animations; the switch shows <UI>ON</UI> or <UI>OFF</UI>.

## Support

{/* claim: collect-support */}
The <UI>Support</UI> tab, headed <UI>Help Center</UI>, has:

- <UI>Contact us / Feedback</UI> — type a message and tap <UI>Send Mail</UI>; it opens an email to info@puente-dr.org in your mail app.
- <UI>Rate our app</UI> — opens the store's rating prompt.
- <UI>Delete user?</UI> — explains that records you already submitted belong to your organization and are not removed, then opens the account page in your browser, where you can request deletion. See [Delete your account](/docs/privacy/delete-your-account/).

## Log out

{/* claim: collect-logout-guard */}
<UI>Log out</UI> is near the bottom of both tabs. It asks <UI>Log out?</UI> and reminds you that you need your username and password to sign back in.

- If forms are still waiting to send, the question says how many, and that you cannot sync them until you sign back in.
- Offline, Collect refuses with <UI>Can't log out right now</UI>, because signing back in needs a connection.

<Aside type="caution" title="Send everything first">
Open the cloud and send your forms until it reads <UI>No forms queued. You are all caught up!</UI> before you log out or lend the phone. See [Offline and sync](/docs/collect/offline-sync/).
</Aside>

## Version

{/* claim: collect-version-label */}
The last line of the Settings tab shows the app version and build, for example "Version 15.7.5" followed by the build number in brackets. Include it whenever you report a problem.

## FAQ

{/* claim: collect-org-change */}
**Where do I change my organization?**
You can't from the app. Email info@puente-dr.org.

{/* claim: collect-offline-records */}
**Does Clear downloaded records delete my unsent forms?**
No. It only clears the residents saved for offline search.
