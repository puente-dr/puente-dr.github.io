Puente Manage is the web app where you look after what your team collects on their phones. In your first week you will mostly do four things: check what arrived, compare it with what the team says they did, fix small mistakes, and download a file. This guide walks through each one in the order you will need them.

## Sign in

{/* claim: guide-manage-signin */}
Open [puente-manage.vercel.app](https://puente-manage.vercel.app) on a computer. Your account is the same one you use in Collect: enter your email or phone number and your password.

<Shot src={login} alt="The Puente Manage sign-in page with Email or phone number and Password fields, a Sign in button, Forgot password and Create account links, and a yellow note telling field workers to open Puente Collect instead." caption="One account for both apps. Manage is for the office; Collect is for the field." />

## Find your way around the menu

{/* claim: guide-manage-menu */}
The menu on the left has six places:

| Menu item | Opens | What you do there |
|---|---|---|
| <UI>Manage</UI> | Form Manager | See every form, how many arrived, and export |
| <UI>Create</UI> | Form Creator | Build or edit a form |
| <UI>Clean</UI> | Data Curation | Search, fix and de-duplicate records |
| <UI>Market</UI> | Form Marketplace | Copy a ready-made form |
| <UI>Review</UI> | Dashboard | See what needs attention |
| <UI>Settings</UI> | Account Settings | Your name, phone, email and password |

<UI>Account</UI> and <UI>Log out</UI> sit at the bottom.

## Check what arrived each evening

Make this a habit for the first week: after the team syncs, spend ten minutes in Manage.

{/* claim: guide-review-dashboard */}
Start at <UI>Review</UI>. <UI>Needs attention</UI> lists problems in recent records, such as <UI>records missing key fields</UI> or <UI>records with an unresolved household link</UI>, each with a label for how serious it is. <UI>Community coverage</UI> shows which communities records came from.

<Shot src={review} alt="The Review dashboard: Needs attention shows 5 of 48 records with an unresolved household link, marked Needs repair, and 5 of 48 records missing key fields, marked Fixable; Community coverage lists six communities." caption="Read Needs attention every evening in the first week. Problems are cheapest to fix while the team still remembers the visit." />

{/* claim: guide-forms-collected */}
Then open <UI>Manage</UI>. Expand a form's row with the arrow and press <UI>Refresh</UI> to see <UI>Forms collected</UI>. Compare it with what the team reported. If it is lower, a phone has not synced yet.

{/* claim: guide-builtin-names */}
Puente's four forms are listed by their internal names: `SurveyData` is Resident ID, `HistoryEnvironmentalHealth` is Environmental Health, `Vitals` is Vitals, and `EvaluationMedical` is Medical Evaluation. Your own forms are under <UI>Custom forms</UI>.

<Shot src={formManager} alt="Form Manager with Puente Forms SurveyData, HistoryEnvironmentalHealth, Vitals and EvaluationMedical, each with an Export button, and Custom Forms below with Edit, Duplicate, Delete and Export." caption="Each row has its own Export button." />

## Clean the records

{/* claim: guide-data-curation */}
<UI>Clean</UI> opens Data Curation. Across the top it counts the records, their average completeness, duplicates and anomalies. Filter by surveyor or community, and open a record to correct it. The <UI>Community Audit</UI> tab groups spellings of the same community so you can rename them to one.

<Shot src={curation} alt="Data Curation with the Records tab open: 48 records, 92% avg completeness, 0 duplicates, 0 anomalies, filters for surveyors and communities, and a table of records with completeness bars." caption="Fix what the team got wrong while you can still ask them about it." />

<Aside type="caution" title="Some changes cannot be undone">
{/* claim: guide-curation-permanent */}
An edit changes the record for everyone in your organization. Deleting a duplicate, and renaming a community's records, cannot be undone. When in doubt, ask the surveyor first.
</Aside>

## Make your first export

{/* claim: guide-export-csv */}
In <UI>Manage</UI>, press <UI>Export</UI> on a form's row. Manage downloads a CSV file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets. If nothing has arrived yet, it says <UI>This form has no records to export yet.</UI>

{/* claim: guide-export-identified */}
The file contains people's names, ID numbers, phone numbers, birth dates and locations. Keep it on a computer only you use, and never send it by email.

## Read the export

Each form gives its own file, with one row for each time the form was filled in and one column for each question.

{/* claim: guide-export-joined */}
- `SurveyData` is the people file: count people there, not in the other files.
- A custom form's export joins every answer to the whole person record, so each row also carries the person's details.

{/* claim: guide-accents-stripped */}
- Custom-form exports remove accents from answers and column headings: `El Río` becomes `El Rio`.

More on columns and joining files: [Reading export files](/docs/data/reading-export-files/).

## FAQ

{/* claim: guide-org-members-can-work */}
**Can every member of my team do this?**
Yes. Any member of your organization can use Manage and download full exports. Invite only people who should see that data.

**The count is lower than the team said.**
A phone still has forms waiting. Ask the surveyor to open the cloud and tap Retry on wifi.

{/* claim: guide-export-failed */}
**The export says it could not be completed.**
<UI>The export could not be completed. Please try again.</UI> Try once more; if it keeps happening, email info@puente-dr.org with the form's name.
