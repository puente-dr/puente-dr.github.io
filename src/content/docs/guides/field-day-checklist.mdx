Use this list on every field day. It is built around pause points: moments when the team stops for a minute and checks a few things before moving on. Most of it protects one gap — between saving a form on a phone and that form reaching Puente, when the phone holds the only copy.

Surveyors do the phone items. The coordinator does the Manage items and checks that every phone is done. Tick the boxes as you go.

## The day before, with internet

These need a connection, so do them at the office or at home.

{/* claim: guide-update-version */}
- [ ] Update Puente Collect from the App Store or Google Play (the current version is 15.7.5)

{/* claim: guide-signin-internet */}
- [ ] Sign in on every phone that will collect — signing in needs internet

{/* claim: guide-forms-reach-phones */}
- [ ] Under the folder, tap the refresh arrow beside <UI>Custom Forms</UI> and check that today's forms are listed

{/* claim: guide-search-offline */}
- [ ] Open the magnifying glass once, so the phone saves the residents for offline search

{/* claim: guide-storage-limit */}
- [ ] If your organization has more residents than that, check the gear → <UI>Find Records</UI> → <UI>Record storage limit</UI> (it starts at 2,000)
- [ ] Charge every phone; pack chargers and power banks
- [ ] Print the paper sign-in sheet, and spare paper forms in case a phone fails

## Before you leave

- [ ] Every phone is signed in under the right person's own account
- [ ] Every phone shows the forms for the day
- [ ] Everyone knows who to call if a phone stops working
- [ ] Everyone knows the plan for the household section of the Resident ID form

## During the visits

{/* claim: guide-search-one-field */}
- [ ] Search before registering: surname alone, then first name, then nickname

{/* claim: guide-consent */}
- [ ] Tick <UI>Community member agrees to data policy</UI> only when the person has agreed

{/* claim: guide-location-button */}
- [ ] Tap <UI>Get Location</UI> at the home, and tap again if it shows `(0.00, 0.00)`

{/* claim: guide-save-messages */}
- [ ] Read the message after each submit: <UI>Saved offline — will sync when you reconnect</UI> means the form is still on the phone
- [ ] Write every person on the paper sign-in sheet

<Aside type="caution" title="Three things never to do in the field">
{/* claim: guide-logout-offline */}
Never log out, never clear the app's data, never uninstall. Forms that have not been sent live inside the app, and signing back in needs a connection.
</Aside>

## Each evening

{/* claim: guide-sync-not-automatic */}
- [ ] Connect each phone to reliable wifi, open the cloud (<UI>Offline Sync</UI>) and tap <UI>Retry</UI> — sending is not automatic

{/* claim: guide-caught-up */}
- [ ] Wait until it reads <UI>No forms queued. You are all caught up!</UI>

{/* claim: guide-households-not-listed */}
- [ ] If anyone created a household offline and the screen shows nothing waiting, keep that phone as it is and tell the coordinator: households are not listed there and are sent only with other forms

{/* claim: guide-still-on-device */}
- [ ] If a form stays in the list, leave it — <UI>Your forms are still saved on this device. Nothing has been lost.</UI> Try again on a better connection
- [ ] The coordinator marks each phone as done

## After the round, in Manage

{/* claim: guide-forms-collected */}
- [ ] In <UI>Manage</UI>, expand each form's row and press <UI>Refresh</UI>; compare <UI>Forms collected</UI> with the sign-in sheet

{/* claim: guide-review-dashboard */}
- [ ] Open <UI>Review</UI> and look at <UI>Needs attention</UI>
- [ ] Note any person registered twice, for the coordinator to fix
- [ ] Write down what went wrong and what to change next time

## FAQ

**Do we need every item every day?**
The evening sync, yes, every day. The day-before list matters most before the first day of a round.

{/* claim: guide-discard-permanent */}
**A form keeps failing. Can we discard it?**
Only with the coordinator, and only after copying its answers to paper. Discarding deletes it from the phone permanently.
