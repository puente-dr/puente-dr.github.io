Training is where a team learns the two habits that keep data safe: search before you register, and sync every evening. This guide covers who trains, how to practise without leaving invented people in your data, a session plan, and a small pilot before the first real round.

## Who trains

Pick one person to be the team's tech lead: the first one anyone asks about the app. Train that person first, on their own, before the rest of the team. They prepare the phones, run the evening sync and answer questions in the field.

{/* claim: guide-dhis2-support */}
That order follows the DHIS2 implementation guidance, which says the IT support unit should be trained before the first end user.

{/* claim: guide-dhis2-devices */}
Train on the phones people will carry. DHIS2's guidance says the same: "If possible, train users on the devices they will use in their work". Each person signs up with their own account, on their own phone, during the session.

## Practise in airplane mode

Practising with signal sends invented people straight into your organization's records. Practise with no signal instead, and throw the practice forms away before anyone syncs.

{/* claim: guide-practice-discard */}
1. Turn on airplane mode.
2. Fill in a form for an invented person and submit. The banner and <UI>Saved offline — will sync when you reconnect</UI> appear.
3. Open the cloud (<UI>Offline Sync</UI>). The practice form is listed there.
4. Tap <UI>Discard</UI>, then <UI>Discard permanently</UI>.
5. Only then turn airplane mode off.

## A 90-minute session

| Time | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 min | Why it matters | One form, one person, one phone: until it is sent, the phone is the only copy |
| 10–25 min | Sign up and sign in | Everyone, on their own phone, with signal; pick the organization from the suggestions |
| 25–40 min | Find before you register | With signal, pairs search for people already in your organization's records by surname, first name and nickname |
| 40–65 min | A full visit offline | Airplane mode on: consent, Resident ID with Do Nothing, one more form, then discard |
| 65–80 min | The evening sync | With signal: open the cloud, tap Retry, read the result |
| 80–90 min | Questions | Go through the field day checklist together |

{/* claim: guide-search-one-field */}
Spend real time on searching. With a connection, the search matches the start of one field, so a full name typed together finds nobody — the most common reason people register someone twice.

## Run a small pilot

{/* claim: guide-dhis2-pilot */}
Before the whole team goes out, send two or three trained people for one short day with the real form. DHIS2's advice is to "Finalize and pilot the tracker before scaling it"; the same holds for a Puente form.

The pilot's data is real, so the people visited must consent and the records stay.

## What to check after the pilot

{/* claim: guide-forms-collected */}
- In Form Manager (<UI>Manage</UI>), expand the form's row, press <UI>Refresh</UI>, and compare <UI>Forms collected</UI> with the paper sign-in sheet.

{/* claim: guide-review-dashboard */}
- Open <UI>Review</UI>. Read <UI>Needs attention</UI>, such as <UI>records missing key fields</UI>.
- Export the form and read the file. Is every question a column you can use?
- Ask the pilot team what confused them, and fix the form before the round.

## Keep a trained list

Keep a simple list: name, phone, date trained, whether they passed the airplane-mode practice, and whether they have done a pilot day. Only people on the list go out alone.

{/* claim: guide-org-deactivate */}
When someone leaves the team, take them off the list and <UI>Deactivate</UI> them on the Organizations screen. That ends their current sessions. When someone leaves, also tell Puente at info@puente-dr.org.
