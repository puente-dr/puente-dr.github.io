A clinic often has no signal. That is fine: Collect saves each form on the phone and sends it later. But between saving and sending, the form exists in one place only — that phone. Most of this guide protects that gap. It is written for the coordinator and the on-site tech lead; surveyors should read the parts marked for them.

## Who does what

| Person | Job |
|---|---|
| **Coordinator** | Owns the form, the paper sign-in sheet and the final count. Talks to the research team. |
| **Tech lead** | Prepares every phone, runs the evening sync, and is the first person anyone asks about the app. |
| **Surveyors** | Search, register, fill in the exam form. Never log out. |

## Two days before

Do this on every phone that will collect, somewhere with a working internet connection.

{/* claim: guide-update-version */}
1. **Update Puente Collect** from the App Store or Google Play. The current version is 15.7.5.

{/* claim: guide-signin-internet */}
2. **Sign in** with each surveyor's own account. Signing in needs internet, so do it now, not at the clinic.

{/* claim: guide-forms-reach-phones */}
3. **Check the exam form is there.** Under the folder, tap the refresh arrow beside <UI>Custom Forms</UI>.

{/* claim: guide-practice-discard */}
4. **Test without signal.** Turn on airplane mode, fill in the form for an invented patient, and submit. Open the cloud (<UI>Offline Sync</UI>), tap <UI>Discard</UI> and then <UI>Discard permanently</UI>, so the invented patient never reaches the data.
5. **Write the phone on a list:** who carries it, and whether it passed the test.

Bring chargers, and one power bank for every two phones. A phone with a flat battery keeps its forms, but it cannot collect new ones.

## Search before you register

For surveyors. The most common mistake at a clinic is registering someone who is already in Puente. The app does not warn you; the patient ends up as two people, with their visits split between them.

{/* claim: guide-search-one-field */}
Search by surname alone, or by the first digits of the cédula. A full name typed together finds nobody. More ways to search: [Find records](/docs/collect/find-records/).

## During the clinic

For surveyors.

- **One exam form per patient visit.** Everything measured at that visit goes in the same form.

{/* claim: guide-save-messages */}
- **The offline banner is normal.** <UI>You're offline. Records will save and sync automatically.</UI> Keep working.

{/* claim: guide-queue-on-device */}
- **Never log out, clear the app's data or uninstall it.** Forms that have not been sent are stored inside the app.
- **If a patient does not consent,** answer "No" to the consent question, and follow the clinic's paper process for their care.
- **Write every patient on the paper sign-in sheet.** It is how the coordinator checks, later, that nobody was lost.

## Each evening

{/* claim: guide-sync-not-automatic */}
1. Connect every phone to a reliable wifi network.
2. Open the cloud (<UI>Offline Sync</UI>) and tap <UI>Retry</UI>. Nothing is sent until you do.

{/* claim: guide-caught-up */}
3. Wait until the screen says <UI>No forms queued. You are all caught up!</UI>
4. The tech lead marks the phone as done on the list. A phone that isn't marked isn't finished for the day.

<Shot phone src={queue} alt="The Offline Sync screen listing one queued Resident ID form, with a Discard link and a Retry button." caption="Still one form on this phone. Tap Retry, not Discard." />

{/* claim: guide-forms-collected */}
Then, on a computer, check the total. In Manage, open <UI>Manage</UI>, expand the exam form's row with the arrow, and press <UI>Refresh</UI>. Compare <UI>Forms collected</UI> with the sign-in sheet.

## When something breaks

{/* claim: guide-still-on-device */}
{/* claim: guide-logout-offline */}
| What happened | What to do |
|---|---|
| A form stays in the list after Retry. | It is still on the phone: <UI>Your forms are still saved on this device. Nothing has been lost.</UI> Try again on a better connection. Don't discard it. |
| <UI>The server did not accept these forms. Try again in a few minutes, and contact support if it keeps happening.</UI> | Wait and try again. If the same forms keep failing on good wifi, report it. |
| A phone's battery died. | Charge it and open the app again. Its forms are still there. Don't reinstall. |
| Someone wants to log out with forms waiting. | The app warns them, and blocks it with no signal. Sync first, on wifi. |
| A patient was registered twice. | Write both names on the sign-in sheet and carry on. The coordinator fixes it after the clinic. |

<Aside type="caution" title="Discarding is permanent">
{/* claim: guide-discard-permanent */}
Discard deletes a form from the phone and it cannot be recovered. Use it for the test patient before the clinic, never for a real one.
</Aside>

## After the clinic

1. Run the evening sync one last time on every phone.
2. Compare the total in Form Manager with the sign-in sheet. If they differ, find out why before sending anything.
3. Then follow [Sharing data with researchers](/docs/guides/sharing-data-with-researchers/).

Write to [info@puente-dr.org](mailto:info@puente-dr.org) only if no phone can save forms at all, or a sync seems to have lost forms. Say which phone, what time, and attach a photo of the screen.
